Active Research Areas

MIGS Outcomes in Bangladeshi Eyes

Building the local evidence base — long-term outcomes of angle surgery and modified techniques in a South Asian population.

Techniques and devices engineered for affordability without compromising safety — from tFlow to instrument-minimal methods.
