Welcome

Interventional Glaucomatologist. Surgeon. Mentor. Researcher. Innovator.

Dr. Md Iftekher Iqbal is an internationally recognized glaucoma specialist and the pioneer of Minimally Invasive Glaucoma Surgery (MIGS) in Bangladesh — the first surgeon in the country to introduce TrabEx Pro, GATT, and BANG, and the first Bangladeshi to publish MIGS cases in internationally indexed journals.

He brings particular skill to complex glaucoma and cataract surgery, managing challenging cases and complications from previous surgery with advanced techniques, guided by evidence and by what is genuinely safe and effective for each patient.

  • MBBSIbrahim Medical College, Dhaka — 2010
  • MS (Ophthalmology)Bangabandhu Sheikh Mujib Medical University — 2019
  • Long-term Glaucoma FellowshipIspahani Islamia Eye Institute and Hospital, Dhaka
