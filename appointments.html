For Patients

What to Bring to Your Visit

Previous Records

All prior prescriptions, eye-drop bottles you currently use, visual field reports, OCT scans, and operative notes if you have had surgery before.

Time for Examination

A thorough glaucoma evaluation may include pressure measurement, gonioscopy, and dilated examination — please allow adequate time for your visit.

Your Questions

Write your questions down beforehand. An informed patient makes better decisions, and no question about your eyes is too small.
