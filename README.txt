DR. MD IFTEKHER IQBAL — OFFICIAL WEBSITE
=========================================

WHAT IS IN THIS FOLDER
  index.html          Home page
  about.html          Biography, honors, timeline
  innovations.html    Named techniques & complex surgery
  research.html       Publications & research profiles
  teaching.html       MIIQ series, mentorship, media
  appointments.html   Chambers, hours, phone numbers
  css/style.css       All colours & styling (edit brand colours here, once)
  img/bg-*.svg        Branded background art for each page header

WHAT TO ADD YOURSELF (optional but recommended)
  photo.jpg                       Your portrait (square, shown on Home)
  img/hero-home.jpg               OR photo behind the Home header
  img/hero-about.jpg              Photo behind the About header
  img/hero-innovations.jpg        Photo behind the Innovations header
  img/hero-research.jpg           Photo behind the Research header
  img/hero-teaching.jpg           Photo behind the Teaching header
  img/hero-appointments.jpg       Photo behind the Appointments header

  Use wide photos (1600px or wider). The moment a hero-*.jpg file
  exists, it automatically appears behind that page's header with
  the dark overlay keeping text readable. If absent, the branded
  gold artwork shows instead. Nothing else to change.

HOW TO EDIT
  Open any .html file, find the comments marked "EDIT HERE",
  and change only the text between tags. Full instructions are
  in HOSTING-GUIDE.md.
